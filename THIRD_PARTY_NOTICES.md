# Content attribution

Existing corpus source: Vedic Scriptures Bhagavad Gita API, https://github.com/vedicscriptures/bhagavad-gita-api and https://vedicscriptures.github.io/terms-and-conditions.html.

English translation: Swami Sivananda. Hindi translation: Swami Tejomayananda. Sanskrit/transliteration and chapter summaries: preexisting project corpus attributed to Vedic Scriptures. Retained translations may contain historical spellings or source transcription errors.

Modern commentary and translations are not automatically public domain because the Sanskrit source is ancient. Attribution is preserved. The upstream repository identifies GPL-3.0 for its software; this should not be interpreted as independent evidence of rights to every third-party translation. No blanket public-domain claim is made. Long purports and third-party word glosses are excluded from served data.

Life-topic connections, prompts and daily reflections are new modern interpretations for this app and clearly distinguished from quoted scripture. They are not medical, legal, financial, or crisis counseling.

The bilingual shloka explanations added in this revision are original contemporary study notes. They are not attributed to the translators. All 701 entries are covered by 148 passage notes; the applicable verse range is displayed with each explanation.
