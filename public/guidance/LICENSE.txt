This site distributes a modified subset of the multilingual static embedding
table: English/Hindi vocabulary selection, per-row 4-bit quantization, and
precomputed 8-bit scripture vectors. All 256 semantic dimensions are retained.
It is not the original full model and its retrieval behaviour differs.
